VYO Education

"Nurturing compassionate leaders of tomorrow who are proud of their roots, heritage, and culture."

Our program offers a unique learning experience designed to help children (ages 6-16) develop an appreciation for India’s rich heritage. We focus on building a strong spiritual foundation through Pushtimarg teachings, ensuring our youth grow with a sense of religious identity and linguistic pride.

Class Schedule

  • 🕒 Timing: Biweekly Sundays @ 11:30 AM
  • 🏫 Format: Instructor-Led Sessions
  • 📚 Curriculum: VYO Structured Lessons
  • 🎨 Creative: Cultural Arts & Crafts

What We Teach

  • Hindu Values & Festivals
  • Pushtimarg literature
  • Indian History & Geography
