Our Education Mission

"Nurturing roots, inspiring growth."

Our primary mission is to provide a vibrant platform where children and youth can connect deeply with the teachings of Pushtimarg.

We are dedicated to preserving and passing on the rich heritage of Indian and Gujarati culture, ensuring that our younger generation grows with a strong spiritual foundation, linguistic pride, and a lifelong bond with our traditions.
